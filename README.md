# Hi, I'm Dr. Ghulam Mustafa 👋

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Interdisciplinary Research Center for Industrial Nuclear Energy (IRC-INE), King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals (KFUPM), Saudi Arabia.

My research combines advanced composite materials, physics-based computational modeling, machine learning, and nuclear energy applications. I develop experimentally validated mathematical and data-driven models for complex engineering systems.
